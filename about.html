I'm Margaret, a New York-based calligrapher and personalization artist.

When I was in grade school, my father taught me brush calligraphy, and since then I was enamored. I experimented with different lettering styles and calligraphy scripts, writing on any surface I could get my hands on. You'll often find me perusing stationery stores when traveling around the world.

Having worked as an art director, product designer, and calligrapher, my background has centered around delivering beautiful finished pieces for clients.
