I'm Margaret, a New York-based calligrapher and designer.

When I was in grade school, my father taught me brush calligraphy, and since then I was hooked. I experimented with different lettering styles and calligraphy scripts, writing on any surface I could get my hands on. You'll often find me perusing stationery stores when traveling around the world.

Having worked as an art director, product designer, and calligrapher, my background has centered around taking creative prompts and delivering beautiful finished pieces for clients.

My favorite thing about calligraphy is still how the simple act of writing letters by hand never fails to amaze an audience. Who knew learning cursive could be so rewarding?
