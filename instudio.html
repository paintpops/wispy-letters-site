How does it work?

What is your turnaround time?

+

Turnaround time varies depending on the complexity of the project, number of items that need to be calligraphed, and/or current workload. Most projects take 2-4 weeks, while smaller orders can generally be done in a week. Rush orders are available for an additional fee.

Do you provide the materials or do I send mine?

+

For place cards and envelopes, either option is available. Most clients either mail the materials, deliver via messenger, or drop it off themselves at my Long Island studio. I can also source the materials for a fee if needed. I recommend sending 10% extra quantities of materials to account for any ink testing or potential typos.

For engraving and hot foiling, you can directly send me the product you would like customized and I will send it back to you on completion.

How many rounds of revisions are included for spot calligraphy?

+

I offer up to 3 rounds of revisions and digitize only the final version. Additional revisions are available for a fee.
