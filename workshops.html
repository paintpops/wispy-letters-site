The gift of lifelong learning

Having taught hundreds of students since 2018, learn the fundamentals of calligraphy with me through workshops. Each session is intentionally paced, allowing space for both instruction and quiet practice.

Private workshops

Private workshops are great for team bonding events, party organizers looking for a low-key activity, and anyone who wants more focused attention for a small group of students.

Workshops typically run about 2 hours for up to 20 students. I provide all materials that students can then take home for additional practice.
